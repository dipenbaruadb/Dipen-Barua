# Privacy Policy - Taka Manager

Taka Manager collects account and finance data to provide budgeting, transaction tracking, reminders, and secure cloud backup.

## Data We Collect
- Account data: name, email, phone.
- Finance records: transactions, categories, loans, goals, shopping lists.
- Device token: for notifications.

## How We Use Data
- Sync and backup your financial data across your devices.
- Generate reminders and monthly summaries.
- Securely authenticate users.

## Security
- User-level Firebase rules isolate data by UID.
- Sensitive local settings are kept in secure storage.
- Optional biometric and PIN protection.

## Data Deletion
Users may request account/data deletion from app settings/support contact.
